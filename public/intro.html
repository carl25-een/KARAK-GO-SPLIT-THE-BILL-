Welcome to Karak Go

Karak Go is a matcha café — matcha drinks, desserts and café food, priced in KSh. This is our split-the-bill system: add your table's order and it works out exactly what each person owes, so friends and tablemates can split a café bill fairly, down to the last shilling.

Enter — split a bill

How it works

1

Start a bill

Start a bill for your table and add everyone's names.

2

Add what you ordered

Add what you ordered from the café menu — every matcha, dessert and bite.

3

Split & settle up

Split it — equally, by item, or by custom percentages — and settle up who owes whom.

What's inside

  • The full café menu, priced in KSh
  • Tip, tax and discount all handled for you
  • Mark who's paid, so nobody loses track
  • A shareable link for every bill — send it to your table

Got a bill link from your table? It opens your bill directly — no need to start here.
